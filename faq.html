FAQ

The Blessing Haus website is still under construction.

Q: Are staff at The Blessing Haus licensed to practice in Germany?

A: Staff are professionals who have gone through the formal licensing process to practice in their country of origin, but at this time are not allowed to give a clinical diagnosis to clients in Germany. If requested by the client, TBH staff may advocate with general practitioners or psychiatrists licensed within the German medical system for medication or expanded treatment.

Q: How much do sessions cost?

A: Sessions are free, there is no cost.

Q: How do I make a donation to The Blessing Haus?

A: Donations to The Blessing Haus are not encouraged, in accordance with German regulations. If you'd like to inquire further about this policy, pleae reach out to a clinician of The Blessing Haus.

Q: Are sessions faith-based?

A: Sessions incorporate faith to the extent it's helpful for the client. This is a great discussion to have with your clinician!

Q: How early should I arrive for sessions?

A: Please don't arrive early for in-person sessions. To protect your privacy and the privacy of other clients, sessions are scheduled to prevent crossing paths when leaving or arriving at the office. It’s better to arrive a couple minutes late than to arrive a couple minutes early. If your session is online, you may log on as early as you'd like.
